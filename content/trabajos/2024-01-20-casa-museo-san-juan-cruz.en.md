This building of humble origin is located in the Plaza del Templete of Caravaca de la Cruz (Murcia) and according to oral tradition was the dwelling that San Juan de la Cruz inhabited during his travels to the town for the foundation of the Discalced Carmelite convent. Historical documentation indicates that the mystical saint rented "a small house from some Moriscos" in 1586 near the Church of the Immaculate Conception.

## The Building

The property is cataloged in the General Urban Planning Plan of Caravaca de la Cruz with grade 2: structural conservation. The catalog sheet indicates the following in observations: "house where San Juan de la Cruz lived". It has three floors and is a good example of humble residential architecture that has not undergone significant changes since the 18th century: wooden panel carpentry remains and the only preserved wooden projecting balcony in Caravaca de la Cruz.
