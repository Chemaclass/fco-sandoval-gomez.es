This building of humble origin is located in the Templete Square of Caravaca de la Cruz (Murcia) and according to oral tradition was the dwelling that San Juan de la Cruz inhabited during his travels to the locality for the foundation of the Discalced Carmelites convent. Historical documentation indicates that the mystic saint rented "a small house from some Moriscos" in 1586 near the Purísima Concepción church.

## The Building

The property is catalogued in the Urban Planning General Plan of Caravaca de la Cruz with grade 2: structural conservation. The catalogue sheet indicates the following in observations: "house where San Juan de la Cruz lived". It has three floors and is a good example of humble residential architecture that has not undergone significant changes since the 18th century: wooden panel joinery and the only wooden projecting balcony preserved in Caravaca de la Cruz remain.
