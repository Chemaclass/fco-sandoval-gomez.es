San Miniato al Monte is a Romanesque basilica that has experienced the passage of time from a privileged promontory. This building was witness to the construction of the enormous dome of Santa María del Fiore and the city's general transformation. 

It itself changed over time, and although inside we can perceive that characteristic sensation of paleochristian basilicas where the pantocrator mosaic stands out, other ornaments were added. 

The facade employs the same materials as Santa María Novella: white marble combined with green stone from Prato. I don't want to elaborate on its history, there are a thousand websites that talk about it. But I do want to emphasize those small details that go unnoticed. The rectangular window framed in the second body of this Romanesque temple is clearly classical, a design typical of the Renaissance that was added to the facade.
